About the project

Remember the old Roblox website? The simple pages, blue headers, catalog, and blocky avatars. That's the era this project is trying to bring back.

WATRBX Recontinued/Rebased is a PHP-based continuation of WATRBX, a revival inspired by Roblox in 2015 and 2016. It's being worked on by the community, one part at a time.

This is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with ROBLOX Corporation.
